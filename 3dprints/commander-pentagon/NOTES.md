# Commander pentagon tray

**Status: test print in progress, result unknown.** One copy is printing (started
2026-10-17); it is not finished, so fit and print quality are not yet confirmed.

An open tray for five sleeved commander decks (Dragon Shield matte, 100 cards plus about
15 unsleeved tokens each). The decks stand upright, on their short edge, in five slots
around a central dice well. Triangular pockets fill the corners between the slots, and
each slot has finger notches in its inner and outer walls.

- Size: 267 × 254 × 55 mm. Print flat, bottom down; no supports.
- Fit-critical: `deck_thick = 72` is an estimate, not measured from a real deck. Measure
  one before printing.
- Earlier variant: decks lying on their long edge (swap `sleeve_w`/`sleeve_h` in
  `slot_w`, `wall_h = 40`) gave a 308 × 293 × 40 mm tray.

# Laundry sensor case (open tray)

**Status: printed once (2026-11-22), not yet fitted.** The boards and battery holder had not
arrived; board fit, lips and the 3.4 mm insert holes are unconfirmed.

Tray for hardware/laundry-sensor (87 x 37 mm board, Keystone 2462 2xAA holder on the back). The
component side is exposed: buttons, LED, USB-C and the PPK2 jumper are reached directly.
`laundry-case.scad` -> `laundry-case.3mf`; `insert-test.scad` is the coupon that chose `insert_d`. Outer 92.3 x 41.6 x 22.85 mm.

- Print floor down (magnet pockets on the bed); no supports. The two right-end lips overhang 2.5 mm.
- Hardware: 2x M2 x 6 screws into M2 x 4 mm heat-set inserts (3.4 mm holes, from insert-test.3mf),
  4x 10 x 3 mm disc magnets glued in.
- Fit: put the board in tilted, right end first under the lips, lower the left end onto the
  standoffs, screw it down.
- Holder height 16.0 is from the Keystone sheet. Unverified until parts arrive: board pocket clearance,
  lip height over the board.

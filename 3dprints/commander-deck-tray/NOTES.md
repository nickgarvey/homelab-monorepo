# Commander deck tray

**Status: not printed yet.** Replaces the commander pentagon tray with a smaller one.

An open rectangular tray for five commander deck boxes (64 × 71 × 93 mm each), standing
on their 64 × 71 mm face in a 3 + 2 grid with no dividers between them. The sixth grid
spot, back right, is an open compartment for dice and tokens; its two inner walls hold
the neighbouring boxes in place. Grooves in the floor mark each box's spot.

- Size: 196.2 × 146.2 × 32.4 mm. Print flat, open side up; no supports.
- Fit-critical: 0.5 mm clearance per side around each box (`clearance`). Measure a few
  deck boxes before printing; they vary.
- Dice compartment inside: 62.4 × 69.4 mm, full tray depth.
